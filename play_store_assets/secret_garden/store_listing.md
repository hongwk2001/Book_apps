# The Secret Garden - Bilingual Book

## Title
The Secret Garden Bilingual

## Short Description
Enjoy Frances Hodgson Burnett's The Secret Garden in English & Korean with TTS audio.

## Long Description
Discover the magic of Frances Hodgson Burnett's classic, The Secret Garden, in a beautifully designed bilingual reading app! Ideal for language learners and fans of classic literature, this app provides the original English text paired with an accurate Korean translation.

**Key Features:**
* **Bilingual Display:** Experience the story in English and Korean, aligned perfectly for easy comprehension.
* **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Listen to the enchanting tale with built-in TTS capabilities. Adjustable playback speed for learning.
* **Personalized Reading:** Change text size, pick from beautiful themes (including Dark Mode), and read comfortably.
* **Fully Offline:** Read and listen without needing an internet connection.
* **Auto-Save Progress:** Pick up right where you left off with automatic progress tracking.

Unlock the door to the Secret Garden while improving your language skills. Download today and step into a world of literary magic!
