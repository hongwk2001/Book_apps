# Frankenstein - Bilingual Book

## Title
Frankenstein Bilingual Reader

## Short Description
Read Mary Shelley's classic Frankenstein in English and Korean side-by-side with TTS.

## Long Description
Immerse yourself in Mary Shelley's timeless masterpiece, Frankenstein, with our innovative bilingual reading app! Perfect for language learners and classic literature enthusiasts, this app presents the original English text alongside a high-quality Korean translation.

**Key Features:**
* **Bilingual Display:** Read English and Korean side-by-side or paragraph-by-paragraph.
* **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Listen to the story unfold with our built-in TTS feature. Customize the reading speed to suit your preference.
* **Customizable Reading Experience:** Adjust font sizes, switch between light and dark themes, and set the perfect reading environment.
* **Offline Access:** Once downloaded, enjoy the entire book offline anytime, anywhere.
* **Bookmark & Progress Tracking:** Never lose your place. The app automatically saves your reading progress.

Whether you're studying English, practicing Korean, or simply revisiting a beloved classic, the Frankenstein Bilingual Reader is your perfect companion. Download now and bring the classic tale to life!
