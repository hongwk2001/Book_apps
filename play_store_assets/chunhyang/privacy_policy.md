Privacy Policy for 춘향전 Chunhyang: Korean-English (TKProf)

Last updated: October 25, 2026

TKProf built 춘향전 Chunhyang: Korean-English (package com.tkprof.chunhyang) as a free app. The whole book can be read for free; optional in-app tips can be made to support the developer.

Information Collection and Use
The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Reading progress and settings are saved only on your device. Read-aloud uses your device's built-in Android Text-to-Speech engine.

In-app Tips
Optional tips are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to these payments: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions
The app uses notification and media-playback permissions only to show read-aloud controls while the book is being read aloud.

Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
