# Chapter-by-Chapter Alignment Validation

Here is one paragraph extracted from the exact middle of every chapter to verify that the translations remain perfectly aligned.

## Chapter 01 (Sample `P17`)
- **RAW (Source `P17`)**: During the confusion and bewilderment of the second day Mary hid herself in the nursery and was forgotten by everyone. Nobody thought of her, nobody wanted her, and strange things happened of which she knew nothing. Mary alternately cried and slept through the hours. She only knew that people were ill and that she heard mysterious and frightening sounds. Once she crept into the dining-room and found it empty, though a partly finished meal was on the table and chairs and plates looked as if they had been hastily pushed back when the diners rose suddenly for some reason. The child ate some fruit and biscuits, and being thirsty she drank a glass of wine which stood nearly filled. It was sweet, and she did not know how strong it was. Very soon it made her intensely drowsy, and she went back to her nursery and shut herself in again, frightened by cries she heard in the huts and by the hurrying sound of feet. The wine made her so sleepy that she could scarcely keep her eyes open and she lay down on her bed and knew nothing more for a long time.
- **EN (Modern `P17`)**: During the confusion and chaos of the second day, Mary hid in the nursery. Everyone forgot about her. Nobody thought of her, nobody wanted her, and strange things happened that she knew nothing about. Mary cried and slept by turns for hours. She only knew that people were sick and that she heard mysterious, frightening sounds. Once, she crept into the dining room and found it empty. A partly finished meal was on the table. Chairs and plates looked as if they'd been hastily pushed back when the diners suddenly got up for some reason. Mary ate some fruit and biscuits. Feeling thirsty, she drank a glass of wine that was almost full. It was sweet, and she didn't know how strong it was. Very soon, it made her intensely drowsy. She went back to her nursery and shut herself in again. She was frightened by the cries she heard from the huts and the sound of hurrying feet. The wine made her so sleepy that she could barely keep her eyes open. She lay down on her bed and knew nothing more for a long time.
- **KO (Translation `P17`)**: 혼란과 무질서로 가득한 둘째 날 동안, 메리는 아이방에 숨어 있었다. 모두가 메리를 잊어버렸다. 아무도 메리를 생각하지 않았고, 아무도 메리를 찾지 않았다. 메리가 알지 못하는 기이한 일들이 벌어지고 있었다. 메리는 몇 시간 동안 울다 지쳐 잠들기를 반복했다. 메리가 아는 것이라곤 사람들이 앓아누웠다는 사실과, 정체모를 무시무시한 소리들이 들려온다는 것뿐이었다. 한번은 메리가 식당으로 살금살금 몰래 들어가 보았으나 식당은 텅 비어 있었다. 먹다 남은 음식이 식탁 위에 그대로 놓여 있었다. 의자와 접시들은 식사하던 사람들이 갑자기 일어나면서 다급하게 밀쳐둔 듯했다. 메리는 과일과 비스킷을 조금 먹었다. 목이 말라 거의 가득 차 있던 와인 한 잔을 들이켰다. 달콤한 맛이 났기에 그것이 얼마나 독한 술인지는 알지 못했다. 와인은 곧 메리를 몹시 나른하게 만들었다. 메리는 다시 아이방으로 돌아와 문을 꼭 닫아걸었다. 오두막에서 들려오는 비명과 다급하게 달아나는 발자국 소리에 겁이 났다. 와인 기운 때문에 졸음이 쏟아져 눈을 제대로 뜨고 있을 수조차 없었다. 메리는 침대에 누웠고, 그 뒤로 오랫동안 아무것도 알지 못했다.
