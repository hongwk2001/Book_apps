## 3. Decisions

1. **Title:** English only ("Book Marketing 101"). No Korean title for this book. *Decided.*
2. **Names of real people:** Use each person's official Korean spelling, checked before publishing. Keep the English name in brackets on first use. *Decided.*
3. **Tone:** 해요체 throughout (friendly, polite). *Decided.*
4. **"You" (여러분):** Use it sparingly. In Korean, drop the subject wherever the sentence still reads clearly, since Korean often leaves out "you". *Confirmed.*

## 4. Test sentences (use these to learn, then mark status)

For each term, write one English sentence, then translate it yourself before you check the glossary. Mark the term **learned** only when your Korean sentence reads naturally to you.

- "Your hook has to fit in one sentence." → 
- "Test the thumbnail at the size of a postage stamp." →
- "Keep your metadata accurate, or stores will not show your book to the right readers." →
- "Build your email list before launch day." →
- "Read-through is the share of book 1 readers who buy book 2." →
